# Code of Conduct

PaneGlide welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help keep issues, pull requests, and other project spaces useful and welcoming.

- Be respectful and patient. Give constructive feedback about the work.
- Explain disagreements without personal attacks or assumptions about intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Keep discussion relevant and avoid spam or disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict
participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior.
These expectations apply to everyone participating in the project.

To report harmful content, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant
issue, pull request, or comment. See
[GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for reporting routes, including reports to GitHub Support.
